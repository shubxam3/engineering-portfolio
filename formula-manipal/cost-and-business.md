# Cost, budget and business plan

**Formula Manipal (Formula Student) · 2020 – 2022**

A Formula Student team is judged on more than the car. The static events score a cost report and a business plan, and the team itself has to be financed and run like a small company. As team lead I was responsible for the budget; this page covers that side of the work.

## Budget

- **Proposal for 2021 – 2023.** The team submitted a budget of about ₹58.7 lakh (roughly €68,000 at the time) for the combustion car to the university's Student Project Scrutiny Board, with a report and a presentation covering team structure, results, lessons from the previous season, planned work and the financial plan. The year before, no budget had been sanctioned because of the pandemic.
- **Split by subsystem.** Each subsystem head justified their own items; the proposal combined them:

| Subsystem | Share of the proposal |
|---|---|
| Management | 25 % |
| Vehicle dynamics | 21 % |
| Engine | 16 % |
| Electronics and control systems | 16 % |
| Structures | 13 % |
| Transmission | 5 % |
| Aerodynamics and composites | 4 % |

- **Sponsors in kind.** Ten industry partners supported the team with software licences, materials and safety equipment in place of money.

## Cash ledger

University purchasing runs through purchase orders and takes weeks. Small and urgent purchases went through a team cash account, which I held for the 2021/22 season.

- Every purchase and reimbursement was logged with date, vendor and purpose: more than 150 entries in the season, from steel tubes, moulds and electronics to shipping crates for the car.
- A separate euro ledger covered the expenses during the trip to Formula Student Germany 2022.
- The ledger was the basis for claiming the money back from the university.

## Cost report and bill of materials

The Cost and Manufacturing event is worth 100 of the 1000 competition points. The team hands in a costed bill of materials: every part of the car, listed by system, with its material, manufacturing processes, fasteners and tooling. At the event the judges go through it at the car and ask the team to defend the numbers and the manufacturing choices. The team's document runs to well over 100 pages. I worked on the team's cost report and bill of materials.

## Business plan

The Business Plan Presentation is worth 75 points. The team develops a business case around its race car and presents it to judges who act as investors. At Formula Student Germany a short pitch video is submitted first, followed by the live presentation and questions at the event.

| Competition | Business plan result |
|---|---|
| Formula Student Germany 2022 | 6th of 26 teams, 68.9 of 75 points |
| Formula Bharat 2021 (virtual) | 7th |

I worked on the business plan for Formula Student Germany 2022. Results: [official FSG 2022 results](https://www.formulastudent.de/fsg/results/2022/).

## Rules knowledge

Places at the competitions are decided by rules quizzes before a car is built, so the team trained for them like an event: weekly mock tests per rulebook section, written by the subsystem heads.

- **Formula Bharat 2021:** 1st in the Rulebook Test in both the combustion and the electric class.
- **Formula Student Germany 2022:** qualified through the registration quiz for one of the 26 combustion places.

## Studies from the task phase

In my first year (2020) I wrote three short studies on the business side of engineering as part of the team's training programme:

- **Financial risk management.** Types of financial risk, and how a failure mode and effects analysis with risk priority numbers can rank the risks of a project and assign each one a response.
- **Prototype against mass production.** How material, process and volume differ between a one-off part and a series part, and where CNC machining and additive manufacturing fit in prototyping.
- **Lean production.** The seven wastes and five principles, applied to how a student team manufactures.
