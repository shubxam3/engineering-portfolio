# Leading Formula Manipal

**Team Lead, 10/2020 – 12/2022 · Drivetrain Engineer from 10/2019 · Manipal, India**

Formula Manipal is the Formula Student team of Manipal Institute of Technology. I joined the transmission subsystem in my first year and led the team for two seasons, during which it built both a combustion and an electric car.

## Scope

- An interdisciplinary team of about 80 students across mechanical, electrical, aerodynamics and management subsystems.
- Two cars in parallel, one combustion and one electric, through design, manufacturing and testing.
- Responsible for technical compliance with the rules, the project timeline and the budget. See [cost, budget and business plan](cost-and-business.md).

## The cars

- **Combustion car (FMXX1).** Honda CBR600RR, chain drive to the differential, electro-pneumatic shifting and clutching, full aero package. Entered at Formula Student Germany 2022 as car 296.
- **Electric car.** Single EMRAX 208 motor driving the rear wheels through a two-stage chain reduction with eccentric chain tensioning, chosen over a planetary gearbox for lower weight, lower inertia and simpler manufacturing.

## How the team ran

- **Weekly reports.** Every subsystem and every member filed a short weekly report: goals, what was finished, what was not and why. It made slipping tasks visible within a week.
- **Critical-path planning.** Subsystem timelines were linked in one plan, so a late part in one subsystem showed up as a risk for the others.
- **Task phase for recruits.** New members went through a structured programme of reports and small design tasks before joining a subsystem. I went through it myself in 2019/20 and later ran it for the following intakes.
- **Design for manufacturing.** For the transmission I documented the manufacturing route of every part, and maintained the combined tools and fasteners list across subsystems.

## As drivetrain engineer

Design and validation of gearbox, differential and driveshaft components for weight, efficiency and durability, using CAD/CAE and track testing. See the [drivetrain design study](drivetrain-design-study.md) and the [pneumatic shifter study](pneumatic-shifter.md).
